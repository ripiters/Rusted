### Added:
Cut Iron variants: Cut Iron Blocks, Cut Iron Stairs, and Cut Iron Slabs (available in all oxidation and waxed states).
Create mod integration: Full support for Industrial Iron Blocks, Industrial Iron Windows, and Industrial Iron Window Panes (rusting, waxing, scraping, and EMI support).

13 new languages: Spanish, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Czech, Slovak, Japanese, Hindi, Italian, French, Portuguese, Chinese.
New config option: enableCreateCompat to toggle Create mod integration.

### Changed:
Removed Rustable Iron Block: Vanilla Iron Blocks now rust directly using standard copper oxidation mechanics.

Improved particles: Added block breaking particles when scraping rust or unwaxing blocks with an axe (toggleable in config).
